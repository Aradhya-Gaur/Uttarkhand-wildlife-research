# Uttarakhand Wildlife Research

## Research on the Fauna, Wildlife, and Ecosystems of Uttarakhand

This repository presents a **33-page ecological monograph** on Uttarakhand’s fauna, wildlife, forests, protected areas, and ecosystems. The study was prepared with the help of **IonCure Tech. Pvt. Ltd.** and brings together regional ecological information, species documentation, conservation references, and visual material in one accessible catalogue.

> **Note:** The repository name uses the spelling `Uttarkhand-wildlife-research`, while the state is correctly spelled **Uttarakhand**.

## What the catalogue covers

The accompanying PDF, [`uttarakhand_wildlife_catalogue_ioncure.pdf`](uttarakhand_wildlife_catalogue_ioncure.pdf), includes:

- An overview of Uttarakhand’s geographic and ecological significance
- Coverage of all **13 districts** of the state
- Protected-area matrices and information on important wildlife habitats
- Altitudinal forest zones and ecosystem variation from the foothills to the high Himalayas
- Inventories of representative mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, and other fauna
- More than **55 embedded wildlife photographs**
- Conservation references using **IUCN**, **CITES**, and **Wildlife Protection Act (WPA)** categories
- Discussion of habitat pressures and human–wildlife conflict
- Conservation priorities and the importance of community participation

## Why this resource is useful

The catalogue is intended to give readers a clear, consolidated introduction to Uttarakhand’s biodiversity. It can help:

- Students and educators understand the state’s ecological zones and wildlife
- Researchers identify topics for further fieldwork and study
- Conservation practitioners review species and habitat concerns
- Readers explore the relationship between forests, wildlife, local communities, and development
- General audiences learn about Uttarakhand’s natural heritage through concise explanations and photographs

## How to use this repository

1. Open the PDF catalogue using the link above.
2. Begin with the introductory sections to understand Uttarakhand’s landscape and ecological context.
3. Use the district, protected-area, and forest-zone sections for regional comparisons.
4. Refer to the species and conservation-status sections when studying individual taxa.
5. Use the human–wildlife conflict and conservation sections to connect biodiversity with management challenges.

## Scope and interpretation

This catalogue is an educational and research-oriented compilation. Conservation categories, species names, legal schedules, and population information may change as official assessments and taxonomic research are updated. Readers should consult the latest publications from relevant government departments, the IUCN Red List, CITES, and peer-reviewed literature before using the material for formal policy, legal, or management decisions.

The photographs and other visual material are included to support identification and public understanding; they should not be treated as a substitute for expert field identification.
